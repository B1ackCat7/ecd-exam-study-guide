| Term | Definition | Sources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Economic base** | Industries and activities that bring income into a region by selling to markets outside it or attracting external spending. Economic-base analysis contrasts these with activity serving primarily local demand. | [U.S. Department of Agriculture Economic Research Service — County Typology Codes documentation](https://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products/county-typology-codes/documentation) |

| **Equity** | In accounting, the residual interest in assets after deducting liabilities. In community analysis, fairness that recognizes different circumstances and seeks substantively fair access, process, and distribution of effects.
