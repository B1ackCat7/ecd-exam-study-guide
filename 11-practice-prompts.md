## How to use this file

For short answers:

- choose one of three prompts in a group;
- set a 20-minute timer;
- spend 2 minutes planning, 15 minutes writing, and 3 minutes checking;
- score the response using the rubric below.

For a full simulation:

- create 24 original multiple-choice or true/false questions from the glossary;
- choose one contemporary essay;
- answer one prompt from each of Groups A through H;
- finish with an oral simulation from [12-oral-exam-preparation.md](12-oral-exam-preparation.md).

## Short-answer Group A: Foundations and ethics

1. A councillor asks you to share confidential expansion details before the company has authorized disclosure. Explain how you would respond and preserve the relationship with both parties.
2. A marketing draft uses an employment number that is larger than the official estimate. Develop a process to resolve the disagreement and publish a defensible claim.
3. Explain the role of an economic developer and distinguish professional influence from formal decision authority.

Strong responses should address facts, mandate, public interest, confidentiality, conflicts, accurate evidence, documentation, and communication.

## Group B: Business retention and expansion

1. Design a BR&E program for a community that has not systematically visited local firms.
2. A major employer signals possible closure because of workforce and utility constraints. Develop an immediate and long-term response.
3. Explain how BR&E intelligence should influence workforce, infrastructure, sector, and attraction strategies.

Strong responses should cover objectives, governance, sampling, survey design, visits, confidentiality, issue triage, action plan, feedback, and measures.

## Group C: Attraction, trade, and investment

1. Develop a target-industry process for a small community with limited staff and budget.
2. A change in Canada-US trade conditions affects local exporters and import-dependent firms differently. Explain how you would assess impacts and respond.
3. A site selector gives the community five business days to respond to a detailed project request. Describe the response process.

Strong responses should include readiness, evidence, fit, qualification, project management, confidentiality, partners, timelines, and aftercare.

## Group D: Governance and performance

1. Compare a municipal department, arm's-length corporation, and regional partnership for delivery of an economic development strategy.
2. A program reports many meetings but cannot show outcomes. Build a better performance framework.
3. Council has ten proposed initiatives but resources for three. Develop a transparent priority-setting process.

Strong responses should cover mandate, authority, role clarity, funding, accountability, conflicts, logic model, SMART measures, attribution, and review.

## Group E: Operations and partnerships

1. Design a mentoring program for growth-oriented small businesses.
2. Evaluate whether a public-private partnership is appropriate for an employment-area infrastructure project.
3. A downtown strategy requires municipal, business, institutional, and community participation. Identify participants and assign their roles.

Strong responses should address fit, governance, agreement terms, resources, risk, public interest, rights-holders, RACI, dispute/exit, and outcomes.

## Group F: Communications and engagement

1. Develop a communications plan for a controversial employment-land decision.
2. Prepare an EDO for a media interview following a major plant closure.
3. Design a public engagement process for downtown redevelopment and explain how input will influence the decision.

Strong responses should include objectives, audiences, messages, evidence, channels, accessibility, spokespersons, confidentiality, feedback, timing, and measurement.

## Group G: Planning, marketing, and site selection

1. Explain the strategic economic planning cycle and common reasons strategies fail.
2. Build the content and data-maintenance process for an investment-ready community profile.
3. Explain the EDO's role in land-use planning for a privately owned industrial proposal.

Strong responses should include evidence, engagement, options, action plans, land-use authority, readiness, accurate data, implementation, and monitoring.

## Group H: Finance and impact analysis

1. A business reports profit but cannot meet payroll. Explain what you would review and why.
2. Develop a complete impact-analysis framework for a proposed large industrial facility.
3. Explain how an economic development department should prepare, defend, monitor, and report its annual budget and work plan.

Strong responses should cover statements, cash, relevant ratios, assumptions, fiscal/environmental/socio-economic/transportation effects, scenarios, lifecycle, risk, and referral to qualified professionals.

## Contemporary essays

Set 70 minutes and use a maximum of 3,000 words.

1. Analyze how a major change in Canada-US trade conditions affects different sectors in a chosen Canadian community. Recommend a coordinated response.
2. Analyze how housing availability and affordability constrain economic development and workforce attraction in a chosen region. Recommend actions and measures.
3. Analyze the economic development opportunities and risks of artificial intelligence adoption for local firms and workers.
4. Analyze how a climate-related event or recurring hazard affects the local economy and propose a resilience and recovery strategy.
5. Analyze how a demographic and labour-force shift affects employers, services, housing, and long-term competitiveness.
6. Analyze an Indigenous economic development opportunity and explain how rights, governance, partnership, capacity, financing, and benefits should be addressed.

## Calculation drills

1. A local industry has 900 of 15,000 jobs. The same industry has 30,000 of 750,000 jobs in the reference area. Calculate and interpret the LQ.
2. A local industry grows from 500 to 560 jobs. Total reference employment grows 3%, and reference industry employment grows 8%. Calculate the three shift-share effects.
3. A firm has current assets of 420, inventory of 120, and current liabilities of 210. Calculate current and quick ratios.
4. A firm has cost of goods sold of 800, beginning inventory of 160, and ending inventory of 240. Calculate average inventory and turnover.
5. Average accounts receivable are 125 and annual net credit sales are 1,000. Calculate DSO using 365 days.
6. Interest-bearing debt is 600, assets are 1,000, and equity is 400. Calculate debt-to-assets and interest-bearing debt-to-equity.
7. Sales are 1,500, cost of goods sold is 900, and net income is 90. Calculate gross and net margins.
8. A tactic costs 24,000 and produces 12 qualified leads, 3 proposals, and 1 completed investment. Calculate cost per qualified lead and stage conversions.

Answers:

1. `1.50`; local concentration is 50% above the reference share.
2. Local growth is 12%; reference effect `15`, industry mix `25`, local shift `20`, total change `60`.
3. Current ratio `2.00`; quick ratio about `1.43`.
4. Average inventory `200`; turnover `4.0`.
5. About `45.6 days`.
6. Debt-to-assets `60%`; debt-to-equity `1.50`.
7. Gross margin `40%`; net margin `6%`.
8. `2,000` per qualified lead; lead-to-proposal `25%`; proposal-to-completion about `33.3%`; lead-to-completion about `8.3%`.

## Self-scoring rubric for an 8-mark practice answer

This is an independently created practice rubric:

| Criterion | Marks |
|---|---:|
| Directly answers every part and establishes context | 1 |
| Uses a correct, logical process | 2 |
| Identifies stakeholders, roles, and authority | 1 |
| Uses evidence and recognizes assumptions | 1 |
| Provides implementation, resources, and timing | 1 |
| Addresses ethics, risk, inclusion, and contingency | 1 |
| Defines outputs, outcomes, and follow-up | 1 |
| **Total** | **8** |

After scoring, rewrite only the weakest section. This is faster than rewriting every answer in full.
